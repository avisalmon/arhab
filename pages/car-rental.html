Insurance — what it costs and how to avoid overpaying

  • Check the Israeli credit card first. Coverage varies a lot by card and issuer (Isracard/CAL/Max, or a Visa Signature/World Elite Mastercard/Amex) — ask specifically whether it includes collision damage waiver (CDW) for a rental in the USA, not just "abroad" generally, since terms differ by country and this isn't something to assume either way.
  • The rental counter's own CDW/LDW is the most expensive option — commonly $30–$50/day, which adds up fast over 9–10 days (~$300–$500).
  • Third-party excess/waiver insurance is usually much cheaper: you still carry the rental company's basic coverage, but a policy like insurance4carhire's USA & Canada policy or Allianz's car hire excess insurance reimburses the deductible if something happens — often for well under $100 total instead of $300–500. Verify eligibility as an Israeli resident before relying on either.
  • Liability minimums are mandatory and already built into the US rental price — it's really the collision/damage waiver that's the optional, shoppable piece.
🟡 Family decision: confirm with our card issuer(s) what's actually covered for a US rental, then decide between paying the counter's CDW or buying a cheaper third-party excess policy — don't default to the counter option without comparing.

Crossing into Canada (Day 6, Niagara Falls)

This itinerary does cross the border for one night — Niagara Falls, Ontario side. The good news from checking company policies: most major companies (Enterprise, Hertz, Avis, Alamo, National, Budget) allow driving into Canada with a US rental, and importantly, the restriction on which vehicle classes are allowed typically only kicks in for trips that go further into Canada than Niagara Falls itself — a Niagara Falls, Ontario destination is generally fine with any class, Premium SUV included.

  • Tell the rental company at booking that the car is crossing into Canada — don't just do it unannounced.
  • Ask for the free "Canadian Non-Resident Insurance Card" at pickup (standard at Enterprise for cross-border renters) — keep it with the rental papers.
  • Confirm whether a cross-border fee applies for our specific company/class — this varies and wasn't confirmed in research, so it needs a direct question when booking.
  • Bring passports for everyone for the border crossing (already needed for the trip anyway).
